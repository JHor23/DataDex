# 🔍 DataDex

**DataDex** is a real-time Pokémon Scanner & Overlay utility designed to read on-screen battle nameplates, scan physical cards via camera or screenshots, and deliver instant competitive stats, type matchups, and build insights over any app.

Whether you're grinding battles, checking type coverages on the fly, or browsing all 1,025 Pokémon, DataDex provides instant lookup tools without forcing you to leave your game.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **🖥️ Floating Screen Overlay:** Reads Pokémon battle nameplates live over any active game or app using Android's Display Over Other Apps permission, complete with customizable scan intervals and overlay opacity.
*   **📸 Multi-Mode Scanner & History:** Scan Pokémon directly from your live screen, point your device's camera at trading cards or screens, upload existing screenshots/photos, and review past detections in your Scan History.
*   **📚 Complete DataDex Encyclopedia:** Search and filter all 1,025 Pokémon by name, National Dex number, or elemental type, displaying Base Stat Totals (BST) and instant weakness multipliers.
*   **🕸️ Interactive Type Attribute Graph:** Visualize offensive (`2×`), defensive (`½×`), and vulnerability (`2×`/`4×`) matchups across dual-type and single-type combinations on an interactive radar spoke graph.
*   **⚔️ Instant Battle & Competitive Breakdown:** View detected Pokémon abilities (including Hidden Abilities), popular competitive natures, weaknesses, resistances, immunities, base stat bars, side-by-side comparisons, and recommended builds.
*   **🔊 Audio & Voice Readout:** Toggle subtle lock-on sound effects or hands-free **Voice Output** to speak detected Pokémon names and key weaknesses aloud during battles.

---

## 🔍 Scanner Modes & Customization

DataDex offers three flexible scanning workflows alongside customizable feedback preferences:
*   **Floating Overlay Mode:** Runs an unobtrusive floating scanner over games and apps to detect on-screen text in real time.
*   **Live Camera & Screenshot Scan:** Point your camera at physical cards/displays or pick saved photos for quick identification.
*   **Sample Nameplate Sandbox:** Built-in interactive battle nameplates (e.g., *Charizard • Lv.78*, *Garchomp • Lv.50*) let you test screen reading and stat lookups immediately.
*   **Custom Preferences:** Fine-tune scan intervals, overlay opacity, smart recognition sensitivity, scanner lock-on audio cues, and spoken weakness readouts.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start / Usage

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Build and install the application on your Android device.
3. Grant **Display Over Other Apps** permission to enable live screen reading (or select **Live Camera** or **Scan Screenshot** mode).
4. Launch your game or open the **DataDex Encyclopedia** to search all 1,025 Pokémon and explore the **Type Visualizer**.
5. Customize voice readout, sound effects, and overlay opacity under **Settings**.
